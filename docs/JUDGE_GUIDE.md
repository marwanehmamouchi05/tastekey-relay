# Judge walkthrough

Start with the in-app [Quick tour](https://tastekey.52.47.205.18.sslip.io/tour). It connects the working Personal and Studio journeys without requiring registration. [Published results](RESULTS.md) include a public-list proxy and its negative findings; they do not establish recommendation superiority.

Open [Personal](https://tastekey.52.47.205.18.sslip.io/personal/discover) and [Studio](https://tastekey.52.47.205.18.sslip.io/studio/onboarding) in your own browser. Each Studio visitor gets an empty private workspace. No shared judge account is needed.

## Personal: taste with control

1. Search for Interstellar and explicitly select the intended Qloo result. Add a design taste such as luxury architecture. Search selections carry actual cultural entity IDs; free-text tastes remain useful locally but do not impersonate resolved entities.
2. Open “Help us understand your choice (optional)” to answer a follow-up. Open Your likes and See all likes to edit the list. Guesses stay separate until confirmed. Reject an unwanted guess.
3. Set a practical hotel limit independently of design taste. To exercise live AI, enter “I love luxury architecture. My hotel budget is $60 a night. Keep my movie tastes.” and explicitly choose Interpret with language model. Inspect the proposed changes, then apply them. Only the correction text is submitted for this step.
4. In Suggestions, choose which likes to use for entertainment, shopping or travel. Choose “Share these likes for 24 hours” and inspect the real Qloo results. Choose “Stop sharing” and confirm the results disappear.
5. Travel destinations are inspiration. If you have a genuinely checked accommodation quote, enter its public source, checked time, stay date and USD nightly price. Filtering respects destination, freshness and declared budget. Do not invent a quote to represent actual availability. Shopping's sample catalog is illustrative.
6. In Privacy, export or delete your profile. Common-pool observations are exploratory self-reports, not a controlled study.

## Studio: evidence to an experiment

1. Create an explicitly synthetic cinema brief in Casablanca, with a 500 MAD pilot budget and a one-evening capacity limit. Review Interstellar from cultural search before saving.
2. In Find ideas, choose “Find ideas with Qloo.” Optional AI advisor selection explicitly sends the brief and cultural evidence to Bedrock. Inspect entity IDs, location signal, investigation trace, limits and the four grounded proposals. Allocations are planning bounds, not supplier quotes or financial forecasts.
3. Revise, accept or reject a proposal. Create an action plan from an accepted proposal, then edit its owner, date, objective and dependencies.
4. In More tools → Compare options, compare Rabat and 250 MAD. The city triggers a new location signal and the budget recalculates allocations. Both input snapshots remain attached to their analyses.
5. Outcomes are owner-reported. Enter a result only if it happened; label any test entry synthetic. Later investigations use these reports and rejections under explicit planning rules.
6. More tools → Rate the ideas stores anchored ratings and rationale against an exact analysis ID. An alias does not establish independent participation or blinding.
7. Anonymous demos can generate a one-use private recovery code on the overview. For a secure team workspace, open Account, register/sign in and verify your email. Team supports email-bound invitations, four roles, workspace switching and immediate membership revocation. Claimed team workspaces disable capability recovery. Save exports separately; sign-in does not sync Personal's local graph. See [identity walkthrough](IDENTITY.md).

## Optional website integrations

Open [Use on other websites](https://tastekey.52.47.205.18.sslip.io/integrations). Download the developer extension and follow its manual installation steps. Choose copied likes or filter words, then explicitly filter a supported search-results page. Matching reads current visible card text locally; it does not alter the search engine, validate prices or promise compatibility with every site's current layout. Show all restores the cards.

Try the clearly labeled sample widget to see generic offers. For an actual business setup, sign in as owner/admin, open More tools → Website widget, connect a website while the widget is off, add published offers and then enable it. Copy the generated code to that website. Visitors choose Use my likes, explicitly select labels in the TasteKey popup and approve them. Matching stays inside TasteKey; Stop using my likes resets the order. The sample products/prices are made-up fixtures, not actual inventory or demand. See [integration setup and test boundaries](INTEGRATIONS.md).

## Optional consented audience connection

Create an invitation in Studio's audience view. Open its link in Personal Proof, preview the named business, sign in with a verified account, choose broad categories and explicitly consent. The request contains the invitation, selected category names and consent only; authentication uses the HTTP-only app cookie. It excludes graph nodes, entity IDs, budget and contact information. Withdraw from the participant view. Reports stay suppressed below ten active verified-account contributions; small groups and complements are also suppressed. Anonymous legacy sessions do not count. Team members cannot contribute to their own business audience. Verified accounts do not prove unique people. Do not create fake participants to imply real demand. Automated tests exercise synthetic thresholds; no real customer dataset is claimed.
