# Finalist presentation preparation

## 90-second version

Every new website asks us to start again: What do you like? What should we recommend? Small businesses face the same problem when choosing their next offering or event.

TasteKey Relay connects two useful workflows. Personal keeps an editable list of your interests in your browser. It asks what you mean, lets you correct a guess, and shares only the likes you choose for films, shopping or travel. Loving luxury architecture does not mean having a luxury hotel budget.

Studio helps an independent cinema or similar small business turn cultural evidence into a testable decision. An owner supplies their catalog, goal and budget. Qloo returns real cultural connections. Studio links proposed pilots to that evidence, compares scenarios and saves an action plan. The owner can reject an idea and record what actually happened.

Qloo supplies cultural relationships; TasteKey supplies the permission, clarification and decision workflow. Optional AI helps interpret text, with changes reviewed before application.

We tested the working integrations and published a small public-ballot experiment, including its negative results. It did not prove better recommendations. Our next validation is with real users and business owners. Today, we can demonstrate a working, transparent way to turn cultural data into choices people control.

## Three-minute demonstration

Use the opening above, then follow the [Quick tour](https://tastekey.52.47.205.18.sslip.io/tour):

1. Add the reviewed Interstellar result and answer one optional clarification. Other guesses do not become facts.
2. Add a design taste, then correct only the hotel budget. Inspect and apply the optional model's proposal; unrelated tastes survive.
3. In Suggestions, select specific likes. Request real Qloo results, inspect their source and revoke sharing.
4. In Studio, save an explicitly synthetic cinema brief, goal and budget. Inspect an entity-linked idea, accept it and save a plan.
5. Compare another city and budget. A location signal is not evidence of local ticket demand. Show where actual outcomes would be recorded.
6. Close with the evaluation limit and the next real customer experiment.

Keep extension installation and team setup for optional exploration. If a provider fails, show the error and input-only baseline honestly. Identify a backup recording by its capture date.

## Questions to prepare for

| Question                         | Accurate answer                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Why Qloo instead of only an LLM? | Returned cultural entities/relationships supply inspectable evidence. The model interprets or words ideas; it does not replace the source.                 |
| Are recommendations better?      | Not established. Five public ballots yielded one of 25 held-out titles; fixed simulated verification worsened its rank.                                    |
| Who participated?                | No recruited people. Agents researched public BFI ballots; deterministic title overlap supplied the labels. No filmmaker participated or endorsed the app. |
| First buyer?                     | Independent cinemas/experience businesses testing a program or campaign. This is a target segment, not a paying-customer claim.                            |
| Business model?                  | A Studio subscription or usage allowance is an unvalidated hypothesis requiring customer research and appropriate commercial API authorization.            |
| Are identities unique people?    | Verified email establishes email control, not unique humans or company identity.                                                                           |
| Does a business get my profile?  | Widget likes remain inside a TasteKey frame; audience aggregates require separate consent and suppression thresholds.                                      |
| Do judges need an account?       | Core Personal and Studio use separate private demo sessions. Teams, audience contributions and publishing widgets require verified sign-in.                |
| Are prices/outcomes verified?    | Samples are marked fictional. Quotes and outcomes are user-entered observations, not independently audited inventory or revenue.                           |
| Can it scale?                    | Five concurrent local baseline journeys passed. This does not establish production/provider capacity.                                                      |
