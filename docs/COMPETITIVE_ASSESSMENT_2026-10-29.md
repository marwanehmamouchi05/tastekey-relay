# Competitive context — October 29, 2026

This is a bounded comparison of publicly documented offerings, not hands-on competitor benchmarking or a claim that unmentioned competitor features are absent. The [event gallery](https://qloo.devpost.com/project-gallery) says the organizers have not published it; a ranking against actual competing entries cannot be verified yet.

| Public reference                    | Capability documented by its own site                                                                   | Defensible TasteKey positioning / limitation                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [TasteDive](https://tastedive.com/) | Discovery across music, movies, shows, books and other categories, with lists and reviews               | TasteKey demonstrates an editable uncertainty graph, explicit expiring context grants and a linked small-business planning workflow. This does not establish better discovery quality or exclusive features.                                                      |
| [Qloo](https://www.qloo.com/)       | Cross-category recommendations, taste intelligence, locality and agent grounding                        | Qloo is the cultural-data supplier, not a competitor TasteKey has outperformed. TasteKey adds the permission, reviewed correction, bounded planning and saved outcome workflow on top of returned evidence. Qloo's commercial results are not TasteKey's results. |
| [Tastewise](https://tastewise.io/)  | Food/beverage intelligence using consumer, menu and retail signals, with business-oriented AI workflows | TasteKey is a smaller demonstrable general cultural pilot-planning product, initially aimed at independent cinemas/experience businesses. It does not possess equivalent market/sales datasets, validated demand or enterprise maturity.                          |

The strongest demonstrated combination is an account-free end-to-end Personal and Studio demo, real Qloo evidence, reviewed optional model actions, privacy controls, saved decisions/outcomes and targeted authorization tests. The weakest areas are human recommendation relevance, actual business-owner usefulness, broad browser/merchant rollout, live inventory and commercial validation. A subscription is a hypothesis, not measured willingness to pay.

Avoid “first,” “unique,” “best,” “more accurate,” “sales uplift,” “verified customers,” competitor feature-absence claims or Top 3 predictions. Explain observable behavior and show evidence. A commercially viable next step is a small consented cinema pilot with owner-verified rights/capacity/costs, genuine feedback and an agreed success measure, after confirming commercial API terms. No outreach or customer acquisition has been performed by this assessment.
