# What changes without Qloo, verification or corrections?

These are bounded observations, not a superiority claim. See [RESULTS](RESULTS.md) for actual measurements and negative outcomes.

October 29: on two fresh annual public lists, removing the revised simulated verification hint changes no ranking or metric. Increasing retrieval from 12 to 20 adds one held-out title (1/10 → 2/10), but no top-three gain; it is a research-only option. The no-Qloo editorial catalogue retrieves 3/10, but its same-poll contamination and annual-domain advantage are explicit. See the [full diagnosis](RECOMMENDATION_DIAGNOSIS_2026-10-29.md). The historical table below describes the original experiment, not a rerun of the revised ranking.

| Removed component                                  | Observed or tested change                                                                                                                                                                                         | What this does not prove                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Qloo in Studio                                     | Input-only baseline creates one generic pilot with no cultural entity/evidence links. Three real assisted cases created four entity-linked pilots each.                                                           | More proposals or cultural links do not establish better business advice. No model-only comparison or human usefulness rating was collected. |
| Qloo in Personal                                   | Existing likes, editing, budgets and privacy remain usable; fresh cultural recommendations cannot be generated. Provider failures are surfaced rather than replaced by fabricated live data.                      | A fresh non-Qloo retrieval baseline was not benchmarked.                                                                                     |
| Simulated verification in public-ballot experiment | Qloo order remains intact. Adding the fixed “Emotional storytelling” answer changed candidate order and moved the sole title match from rank 3 to 9.                                                              | The simulation is not a genuine answer by the public person and does not establish harm or benefit for actual user-confirmed answers.        |
| Corrections                                        | A previously entered budget/taste remains unchanged until explicitly edited or a proposed correction is reviewed and applied. Existing tests verify that hotel-budget corrections preserve unrelated tastes.      | Functional correction tests do not measure recommendation-quality recovery. No real-user correction benchmark was performed.                 |
| Context grant                                      | Unauthorized or expired likes cannot enter the corresponding live ranking; revocation discards pending recommendation responses. Privacy's comparison now uses the same production reranker and authorized likes. | Tests cover specified paths, not a general security certification.                                                                           |

The public-ballot comparison freezes identical candidate pools and counts unreturned positives as misses. It conditionally compares ordering inside Qloo retrieval. It must not be presented as proof that Qloo beats an independent LLM, popularity recommender or fixed-question system.
